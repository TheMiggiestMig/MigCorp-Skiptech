# MigCorp - Skiptech

MigCorp - Skiptech adds buildable skipdoors to RimWorld, allowing pawns to instantly teleport between skipdoors on the same map.
Designed for large, sprawling colonies. If your pawns take half a day to walk across the map for a job, build a couple of skipdoors and SkipNet will automatically use them when it makes the trip shorter.
Built standalone for players with or without DLC.

## Features
- **Buildable Skipdoors**  
  Research SkipNet and build your own network of shortcuts around the colony.

- **Automatic SkipNet pathing**  
  Pawns automatically use skipdoors when doing so provides a worthwhile shortcut to an otherwise walkable path. No manual orders required.

- **Manual skipping**  
  Name your skipdoors and manually order pawns to skip between them, including to destinations they couldn't otherwise reach on foot.

- **Powered and unpowered operation**  
  Powered skipdoors work instantly. Unpowered skipdoors can still operate using their internal power cell, but take time to charge and risk giving pawns skip-shock.

- **Archotech Skipdoors**  
  Rare ancient skipdoors can be found which operate instantly without power or side-effects.

- **Customizable behaviour**  
  Configure who can use SkipNet, animal access, skip-shock, unpowered skipdoors, power consumption, and automatic pathing behaviour.

- **Vanilla Psycasts Expanded support**  
  VPE skipdoors can also be connected to and used by SkipNet.
